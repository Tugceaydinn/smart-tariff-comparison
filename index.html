Smart tariff comparison

A working prototype that estimates a household’s annual electricity cost under four tariff structures, from its usage level and when it uses power.

Prototype only. The load profiles are synthetic and the tariff rates are illustrative placeholders, not real supplier prices. The three-band structure (night, day, peak) follows Irish time-of-use design. Do not use for choosing a real tariff.

Method and limitations
  • Annual cost = standing charge × 365 + Σ over 24 hours of (hourly kWh × unit rate for that hour’s band). Prices are shown before VAT and levies.
  • Hourly use = annual usage × a normalised 24-hour shape per household type, plus EV charging spread evenly over the chosen window. Shapes are stylised, with no seasonal or weekday/weekend variation.
  • Tariffs A to D are fictional. Replace the TARIFFS object in the script with current supplier rate cards to test real offers.
  • Next steps: load real half-hourly smart-meter data, add seasonality, test sensitivity to rate changes, and validate results against supplier comparison tools.
